Durban Bunny Chow

1 hr · Serves 4

About the dish

Bunny chow is a street-food icon of Durban, a city with a large South African Indian community. A loaf of bread is hollowed out and filled with a rich Durban curry, so the bread works as both bowl and side. It is traditionally eaten by hand, using the scooped-out bread to mop up the sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken pieces (or lamb), bone-in
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 3 tbsp curry powder (Durban masala if available)
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 unsliced white loaf, cut into quarters
  • Fresh cilantro and salt

Instructions

1. Start the curry base

Heat the oil in a heavy pot and add the cinnamon stick. Cook the onions until golden, then add the garlic and ginger and cook for 2 minutes.

2. Add the spices and meat

Stir in the curry powder and turmeric and cook for 1 minute until fragrant. Add the chicken and coat it in the spices, then add the tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes.

3. Simmer

Add the water and potatoes, season with salt and cover. Simmer for 35 to 40 minutes until the chicken is tender and the sauce is thick.

4. Fill the bread

Hollow out each quarter loaf, keeping the scooped-out bread. Spoon the curry into the hollow, top with cilantro and serve with the bread piece on top.
