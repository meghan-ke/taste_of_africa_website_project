Preserved Lemon Tagine

1 hr 40 min · Serves 4

About the dish

The tagine is named after the cone-lidded clay pot it is cooked in. The shape returns steam to the stew, so the meat stays tender with very little liquid. In Morocco this dish is a Friday and festival favorite, scented with saffron and finished with preserved lemon and olives.

Ingredients

  • 8 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 preserved lemons, rind only, sliced
  • 1 cup green olives
  • 2 onions, grated
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1/2 tsp saffron threads, soaked in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • A handful of fresh cilantro and parsley
  • Couscous, to serve

Instructions

1. Marinate the chicken

Mix the garlic, ginger, turmeric, cumin, saffron water and 1 tbsp olive oil. Rub it over the chicken and leave for at least 30 minutes.

2. Brown and build the base

Heat the remaining oil in a wide pot or tagine base. Brown the chicken on both sides, then set it aside. Cook the grated onions in the same pot for 10 minutes until soft.

3. Slow braise

Return the chicken to the pot, add the stock, cover and simmer on low heat for about 50 minutes, until the chicken is very tender.

4. Add lemon and olives

Add the preserved lemon rind and olives, then cook uncovered for 15 minutes so the sauce thickens. Scatter the herbs over the top and serve with couscous.
