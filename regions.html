Regions

Climate, trade and history shape what each region cooks. Pick a region to see its staples and techniques, then open a dish for the full recipe.

West Africa

Cooking is built on a base of tomato, onion and peppers, with heat from scotch bonnet and richness from palm oil. Meals are often one-pot dishes or stews with a sratch for scooping.

Staples

  • Rice, yam, cassava and plantain
  • Black-eyed peas, groundnuts and fonio
  • Palm oil, scotch bonnet and ginger

Cooking techniques

  • One-pot simmeringof rice in a tomato-pepper base
  • Pounding yam and cassava into fufu
  • Grilling spiced skewers over open flame

Signature dish: Smoky Jollof Rice

North Africa

The food of the Mediterranean coast and the Sahara meets in slow-cooked stews, steamed couscous and fragrant spice blends. Sweet, salty and sour flavors often appear in the same dish.

Staples

  • Couscous, wheat, and chickpeas
  • Olives, dates and preserved lemon
  • Saffron, cumin and ras el hanout

Cooking techniques

  • Slow braisingin a clay tagine
  • Steaming couscous over a simmering stew
  • Preserving lemons in salt

Signature dish: Preserved Lemon Tagine

East Africa

Meals are shared from a large platter, eaten by hand. Ethiopian cooking in particular centers on teff flatbread and richly spiced stews called wot.

Staples

  • Teff, sorghum and maize
  • Lentils, beans and chickpeas
  • Berbere spice and nitter kibbeh (spided butter)

Cooking techniques

  • Fermenting teff batter for injera
  • Baking injera on a large clay griddle
  • Slow-simmering onions into a stew base

Signature dish: Injera & Misir Wot

Southern Africa

Cooking over open fire, know as the braai, is a social tradition. Indian and Malay communities added curry and spice traditions that now sit beside maize porridge and grilled meat.

Staples

  • Maize porridge, beef and game
  • Chakalaka relish and peri-peri
  • Cape Malay curry spices

Cooking techniques

  • Braai: grilling over wood or charcoal
  • Slow-cooking in a cast-iron pot over the fire
  • Serving curry inside a hollowed loaf

Signature dish: Durban Bunny Chow
